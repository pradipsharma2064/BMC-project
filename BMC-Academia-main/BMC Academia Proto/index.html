About Butwal Multiple Campus (BMC)

Butwal Multiple Campus (BUMC), established in Butwal, Rupandehi, Lumbini Province, is one of the premier and historic constituent campuses of Tribhuvan University. Dedicated to academic excellence, innovative pedagogy, and societal development, BMC serves thousands of students across Science, Management, Education, and Law.

Quality & Institutional Stature

  • 🏆 QAA Accredited: Quality Assurance and Accreditation certified constituent institution of Tribhuvan University.
  • 🔬 Research Management Cell (RMC): Active faculty and scholar research cell publishing peer-reviewed e-journals and academic studies.
  • 📚 Comprehensive Campus Library: Rich repository of physical references, TU thesis archives, and digital e-resources.
  • 🌐 Modern Digital Infrastructure: High-speed optical campus networking, smart classrooms, and state-of-the-art computer laboratories.
